# Merrow Therapeutics brand kit

Fictional demo company: a clinical-stage immunology biotech, about 300 staff, in Waltham, MA. Its lead asset is MRW-214, a bispecific antibody for ulcerative colitis. Said "MAIR-oh". Merrow is the client: it issues RFPs, purchase orders and confidentiality agreements to its vendors.

## The mark

The bispecific Y. An antibody is drawn as three separate capsules. The two binding arms take different colours, teal and coral, because MRW-214 binds two targets. The even gaps keep it clinical and crisp, and at 16 px it still reads as a Y. The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans: "Merrow" at 680 in ink, then "Therapeutics" at 400 in teal, on one line.

## Typography

- **Display: Plus Jakarta Sans** (680 for the wordmark, 600 to 700 for titles). It's a calm, modern sans with confident capitals.
- **Body: Albert Sans** (400, with 600 for emphasis). It's clean and quiet, and it reads well in long RFP and contract text.

## Logo use

- Do: give the lockup clear space equal to one arm's width on all sides. Use the white version on Deep Harbor.
- Do: use the symbol alone for avatars, favicons and document corners.
- Don't: swap or match the arm colours (the two colours are the idea), join the three capsules, or set "Therapeutics" in the heavy weight.
- Don't: put the colour lockup on teal or coral grounds.

## Voice

1. Clear and calm. State what we need, by when, and how we will decide.
2. Be a fair client: give vendors the same facts and the same deadline.
3. Patients are the reason. Say so once and plainly, never as decoration.
4. Short sentences and defined terms, with no hype about the science.
