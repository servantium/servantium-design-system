# Avelle Biosciences brand kit

Fictional demo company: a preclinical and clinical lab-services CRO (bioanalysis, immunogenicity, DMPK, biomarkers), about 170 staff, King of Prussia, PA. Said "a-VELL".

## The mark

The meniscus A. It's a contrast A, with a thin left stroke and a heavy right one, so it matches the serif wordmark. Its crossbar is a copper meniscus: the curved liquid surface an analyst reads a measurement from. The idea is precision you can see. The legs are aubergine and the meniscus is copper. The mark stands alone from 24 px up. At 16 px it reads as a plain A, and that's fine.

## Typography

- **Display: Brygada 1918** (600 for titles, 400 for large quotes). It's a sturdy, low-contrast book serif: warm without being decorative.
- **Body: Hanken Grotesk** (400, with 600 for emphasis). It's a clean grotesk with open forms, and it stays legible in dense method tables.
- The `BIOSCIENCES` line in the lockup is Hanken Grotesk 560 with wide tracking.

## Logo use

- Do: give the lockup clear space equal to the meniscus height on all sides. Use the white version on aubergine or on photography darker than 50%.
- Do: use the symbol alone only where the name already appears nearby, for example avatars, favicons and slide corners.
- Don't: recolour the meniscus outside copper, stretch the lockup, set the wordmark in live type, or put the colour lockup on copper or a busy image.
- Don't: drop `BIOSCIENCES` from the lockup. If space is tight, use the symbol.

## Voice

1. Exact before warm. Name the method, the matrix and the timeline.
2. We talk about the sponsor's study, never about our capacity.
3. Make claims a QA auditor would sign: no "world-class", no "cutting-edge".
4. Calm and plain. Short sentences, active voice, with numbers wherever they exist.
