# Lattica brand kit

Fictional demo company: a Series B life-sciences SaaS, about 150 staff, Boston Seaport. It's the shared workspace where biotech sponsors and their CROs track samples and assay data, with AI QC. Said "LAT-ih-ka".

## The mark

An L that comes out of a lattice. On a 3x3 grid, five filled cells spell the L. The centre cell is the shared record where the sponsor and the CRO meet: indigo on light grounds, cyan on dark. The three open cells keep only their lattice point. The mark is built only from squares and dots, so it stays crisp down to 16 px, where the dots fade and the L remains. The wordmark is Sora 600, lowercase, set slightly tight.

Files: `logo.svg/png` (lockup, light grounds), `logo-white.svg/png` (dark grounds), `symbol.svg/png`, `symbol-white.svg/png`, `symbol-64.png` (favicon). The wordmark is outlined, so it needs no fonts.

## Typography

- **Display: Sora** (600 for titles, 700 for big numbers). It's a geometric sans that is wide, confident and engineered.
- **Body: Geist** (400, with 600 for emphasis). It's a neutral, compact UI grotesk with tabular-friendly figures.

## Logo use

- Do: give the lockup clear space of one grid cell on all sides. On dark grounds, use the white version, where the shared cell turns cyan.
- Do: use the symbol alone for app icons, avatars and favicons. On a tinted tile, keep the dark lattice.
- Don't: rearrange or recolour cells, add cells, animate the L apart in static media, or set the wordmark in capitals.
- Don't: put the colour lockup on indigo. Use white on indigo or midnight.

## Voice

1. Direct and specific. Name the record, the plate, the flag.
2. Sponsors and CROs are partners on one record, never "vendors".
3. AI is shown by what it caught, not by what it is.
4. Short sentences, present tense, and no exclamation marks.
